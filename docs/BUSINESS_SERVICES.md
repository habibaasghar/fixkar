# FixKar.pk — Business & Commercial Services Architecture

> **Document Type:** Enterprise & Commercial B2B Framework  
> **Status:** Strategic Architecture (Planning Only)  
> **Target Audience:** Facility Managers, Corporate Offices, Retail Chains, Real Estate Agencies, Restaurants, Educational Institutions, Hospitals  
> **Core Rule:** Never force a high-value corporate account or commercial property manager through a retail 3-field residential handyman booking form.

---

## 1. Commercial Value Proposition & Market Need

In Pakistan, commercial enterprises (corporate offices, retail outlets, banks, restaurants, schools, clinics) suffer from:
1. **Unreliable informal contractors** who lack GST/NTN invoices, corporate compliance, and background checks.
2. **Operational downtime** resulting from reactive, unmanaged breakdowns rather than scheduled preventative maintenance.
3. **Fragmented billing** with separate, unverified vendors for AC, plumbing, janitorial, electrical, and security.

FixKar.pk Business Services serves as a **Single-Vendor Managed Facility Partner**, offering structured service level agreements (SLAs), centralized billing, verified technician teams, and dedicated account management.

## 4. Business Quote Lead Capture Specification

When a commercial customer clicks **"Request a Business Quote"**, the platform captures:

### 1. Company & Contact Details
- **Organization / Company Name**
- **Contact Person Name & Designation** (e.g., Facility Manager, Operations Lead, Owner)
- **Official Work Email**
- **Mobile / WhatsApp Phone Number**
- **NTN / STRN Number** *(Optional at inquiry stage)*

### 2. Location & Facility Profile
- **City** (Lahore, Islamabad, Rawalpindi, Karachi, etc.)
- **Area / Address** (Commercial zone, industrial estate, tech park)
- **Facility Type**:
  - Corporate Office / IT Park
  - Retail Store / Showroom
  - Restaurant / Cafe / Cloud Kitchen
  - Commercial Plaza / Mixed-Use Building
  - School / University / Training Center
  - Healthcare / Clinic / Diagnostic Lab
  - Warehouse / Logistics Hub
- **Approximate Covered Area (Sq. Ft.)**

### 3. Service Scope & Frequency
- **Primary Service Required** (AMC, HVAC, Janitorial, Electrical, Renovation, Multiple)
- **Engagement Model**:
  - One-Time Commercial Job
  - Recurring Monthly Service
  - Comprehensive Annual Maintenance Contract (AMC)
  - Emergency Facility Support
- **Urgency / Expected Start Date**
- **Specific Technical Requirements / RFP Upload** (PDF, DOCX up to 25MB)

## 5. B2B Account Features & Operational Architecture

1. **Corporate Billing & Invoicing:**
   - Formal FBR-compliant Sales Tax and Income Tax invoices.
   - Bank transfer (IBAN) payment terms (Net-15 / Net-30 for vetted accounts).
2. **Dedicated Account Manager:**
   - Single point of contact for multi-location businesses across Pakistani cities.
3. **Escorted & Verified Crews:**
   - Uniformed technicians with photo IDs and supervisor oversight.
   - Non-disruption scheduling (night shifts, weekend work for office environments).
4. **Preventative Maintenance Logs:**
   - Digital asset tagging (e.g., QR-coded AC units) tracking service dates, refrigerant pressures, and filter changes.
