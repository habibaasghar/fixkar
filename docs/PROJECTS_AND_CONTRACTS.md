# FixKar.pk — Projects & High-Value Contracts Framework

> **Document Type:** High-Ticket & Turnkey Project Architecture  
> **Status:** Strategic Architecture (Planning Only)  
> **Target Scope:** Turnkey Renovations, Commercial Fit-Outs, Structural Upgrades & Long-Term Facility Contracts  
> **Core Rule:** High-value construction and remodeling projects involve substantial budgets (PKR 200,000 to 10,000,000+) and extended sales cycles. They must never be treated as single-visit transactional leads.

---

## 1. Project-Based Value Proposition

While standard handyman bookings resolve urgent, small repairs (e.g., tap replacement, socket repair), **Projects & Contracts** represent capital improvements, interior transformations, and ongoing property maintenance retainers.

Customers undertaking these projects require:
1. **Accurate Scope Estimation:** Bill of Quantities (BOQ), material specification sheets, and architectural feasibility.
2. **Milestone-Based Execution:** Phased delivery schedules (Demolition → MEP Rough-In → Surfaces & Finishing → Final Handover).
3. **Financial Protection & Milestone Escrow:** Staged payments tied to inspected completion milestones rather than 100% upfront contractor advances.
4. **Vetted General Contractors & Project Managers:** Skilled master tradesmen with proven project portfolios, monitored by FixKar quality engineers.

## 2. High-Value Project Classification

FixKar.pk structures its project pipeline across 12 distinct contract categories:

1. **Complete Home Renovation** (Full-house architectural remodeling, MEP rewiring/replumbing, modern floor/wall upgrades)
2. **Office Renovation** (Corporate workspace modernizations, executive cabins, acoustic partitions, boardrooms)
3. **Kitchen Renovation** (Custom acrylic/UV cabinetry, quartz/granite counters, concealed plumbing, exhaust systems)
4. **Bathroom Renovation** (Complete gutting, membrane waterproofing, concealed mixer plumbing, imported tile laying, vanities)
5. **False Ceiling Project** (Multi-room gypsum board suspended ceilings, cove lighting design, PVC moisture-proof ceilings)
6. **Flooring Project** (Large-format porcelain tile laying, marble grinding & chemical crystallization, SPC vinyl, laminate wood)
7. **Painting Project** (Full-villa interior/exterior repaint, weather-shield protection, anti-seepage moisture barrier coats)
8. **Electrical Project** (New building full wiring, load distribution panels, industrial 3-phase connections, solar/generator ATS panels)
9. **Plumbing Project** (Complete overhead/underground piping rough-in, sewerage line modernizations, booster pump plant installation)
10. **Commercial Fit-Out** (Shell-and-core office, retail shop, restaurant, or clinic turn-key turnkey delivery)
11. **Property Maintenance Contract** (Multi-property residential or commercial ongoing preventative maintenance retainers)
12. **Construction-Related Projects** (Room additions, boundary walls, structural alterations, porch extensions, civil masonry)

## 5. Risk Mitigation & Contractor Governance

1. **Detailed Scope of Work (SOW):** No project begins without an agreed-upon, signed SOW specifying brand names of materials (e.g., Berger/Jotun for paint; Master/Porta for sanitary; Pakistan Cables for wiring).
2. **Milestone Staged Payments:**
   - 20% Advance for material procurement and site setup.
   - 30% MEP / Rough-in milestone completion.
   - 35% Surfaces and finishing milestone completion.
   - 15% Final punch-list sign-off and warranty handover.
3. **Quality Audits:** FixKar engineers perform physical checkpoint inspections at each stage before milestone funds are released to contractor partners.
