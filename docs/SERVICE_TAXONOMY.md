## Overview & Taxonomy Principles

FixKar.pk organizes its service offering into an 11-category master taxonomy. Each category encompasses specific, high-intent sub-services reflecting how Pakistani residential and commercial customers search, request, and evaluate skilled trades.

### Structural Levels
1. **Master Category:** The macro industry vertical (e.g., `AC & Cooling`, `Electrical Services`).
2. **Sub-Category / Cluster:** Logical group of related services sharing tooling or expertise (e.g., `Sofa Cleaning`, `Ceiling Work`, `Masonry`).
3. **Specific Service / Job Unit:** The exact service requested by a customer (e.g., `AC Gas Refilling`, `DB Panel Work`, `Marble Polishing`).
4. **Service Scope & Intent Type:**
   - **Emergency / Rapid Repair:** Fast same-day handyman response (e.g., short circuit, pipe burst).
   - **Standard Maintenance:** Regular preventative service (e.g., AC general service, water tank cleaning).
   - **Project / High-Value Installation:** Multi-day or quote-driven work (e.g., house painting, kitchen renovation).
   - **Commercial / Contract:** Enterprise facility maintenance and AMCs.
