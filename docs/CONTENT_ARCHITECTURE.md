# FixKar.pk — Content Architecture & Editorial Framework

> **Document Type:** Content Strategy & Modular Editorial Architecture  
> **Status:** Canonical Content Guidelines  
> **Core Principle:** Service pages must be comprehensive, localized, and technically authoritative, while maintaining structural flexibility so that no two pages feel like mechanical clones.

---

## 1. Modular Content Block Architecture

Every future service page draws from a modular library of 15 structured content blocks. Rather than forcing every block into a rigid vertical stack, pages assemble the blocks that best serve their specific trade and customer search intent:

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ 1. Hero & Value Proposition (H1 + Intro + Primary CTA) │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
```

## 2. Block-by-Block Editorial Specifications

### Block 1: The Localized Hero
- **H1:** Must directly mirror user search intent (e.g. *"Professional House Painting Services in Lahore"*).
- **Introduction:** Explains the immediate local context (e.g., addressing monsoon seepage, saltpetre efflorescence, or summer cooling loss). Never use generic fluff.
- **Hero CTA:** Immediate WhatsApp booking trigger with pre-filled message encoding.

### Block 2: Problem Recognition ("Common Issues We Fix")
- Identifies 4–6 specific technical failure symptoms in Pakistani vernacular:
  - *AC:* "Indoor unit water dripping", "Compressor tripping on low voltage", "Blower foul odor".
  - *Plumbing:* "Concealed wall dampness (*seam*)", "Geyser pilot flame extinguishing", "Main line airlock".
  - *Electrical:* "DB box main breaker tripping", "UPS battery not charging", "Flickering SMD lights".

### Block 3: Explicit Service Scope ("What's Included & What's Excluded")
Eliminates customer friction and technician disputes before dispatch:
- **Included:** Technical diagnosis, labor, tool usage, safety testing, clean-up.
- **Excluded:** Replacement parts, copper piping, new refrigerant gas canisters, civil wall plastering (unless specified in quotation).

### Block 4: How FixKar Works (Process Steps)
1. **Request:** Select trade, enter phone number and area.
2. **Confirm:** Quick phone/WhatsApp callback to confirm time slot.
3. **Inspect & Quote:** Technician assesses the job and confirms the price before touching tools.
4. **Fix & Test:** Work completed according to trade safety standards.
5. **Pay After:** Customer inspects and approves before paying directly.

### Block 5: Transparent Pricing Guidance
- Display realistic Pakistani Rupee (PKR) market ranges based on authentic trade audits.
- Explain **Factors Affecting Price**:
  - Material quality (e.g., Berger vs. Jotun vs. Diamond paint; Master vs. local sanitary fittings).
  - Accessibility (e.g., 3rd floor exterior condenser mounting vs. ground floor).
  - Tonnage and capacity (1 Ton vs. 1.5 Ton vs. 2 Ton inverter units).

### Block 6: Trust & Verification Signals
- Reiterate that every technician on this vertical has completed CNIC and trade background screening.
- Highlight the 7-day re-work warranty policy.

### Block 7: Trade-Specific Local FAQs
- Must answer genuine homeowner dilemmas:
  - *"Should I buy the spare parts myself or let the plumber source them?"*
  - *"Can an inverter AC PCB be repaired, or does it require full board replacement?"*
  - *"How long does sofa drying take after steam cleaning during humid weather?"*

## 3. Tone of Voice & Pakistani Localization Lexicon

FixKar's editorial voice is **authoritative, clear, respectful, and pragmatic**:
* **Use Authentic Local Terminology:**
  - Refer to *geysers* (not "water heaters"), *UPS / inverters* (not "battery backup arrays"), *split ACs* (not "mini-splits"), *marlas / kanals* (for land/room size), *water motors / donkey pumps* (for suction pumps).
* **Respectful & Direct Communication:**
  - Avoid overly complex academic English. Keep reading levels accessible to bilingual Pakistani homeowners.
  - Emphasize safety, family privacy, and zero upfront payment risks.
